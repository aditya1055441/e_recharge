Enforced Access Control Rules

  • Master Distributors access only their assigned Distributors and Retailers.
  • Distributors access only their directly assigned Retailers.
  • Retailers cannot modify commissions, adjust wallets, or view upstream credentials.
  • Double-entry ledger balances cannot be directly edited via hierarchy views.
